Our story

Built by runners,
for runners.

STRYDE started in 2026 in a small workshop in Kochi, India — two engineers who were tired of watches that quit before the finish line. The first prototype was handed to a group of ultramarathon runners training in the Western Ghats. They never gave it back.

Today, STRYDE is worn by competitive runners across 40 countries, from 5Ks in Mumbai to 100-mile mountain races in the Alps. Every product decision starts with one question: does this make the run better?
